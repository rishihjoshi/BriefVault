# DailyPulse — Brand Voice

A lightweight voice guide for a personal daily-briefing PWA. Keep it short; this is a
utility, not a marketing site.

## Personality

**A calm, trustworthy morning editor.** Knows what matters, says it plainly, and gets out
of your way. Never breathless, never clickbait.

## Voice attributes

**Concise**
- We are: short, scannable, one idea per line.
- We are not: chatty, padded, or clever for its own sake.
- Sounds like: "No stories yet — check back after the next update."
- Not: "Oops! Looks like we couldn't find anything for you right now 😅"

**Neutral on the news**
- We are: factual and even-handed when surfacing headlines; editorializing only in the
  one-line "why it matters".
- We are not: opinionated, alarmist, or partisan.

**Quietly warm (microlearning only)**
- We are: encouraging and practical in the Learn tab ("Try this: …").
- We are not: motivational-poster hype.

## Style rules

- Sentence case for UI copy and headings; title case only for proper nouns.
- No exclamation marks in system/UI copy.
- En dash for ranges and pairings: **US–India**, "6am–6pm".
- "precipitation", not "precip"; spell out abbreviations.
- Ellipsis only for genuine loading/continuation ("Loading your briefing…").
- Blame-free errors: state what happened + what to do, never blame the user.

## Terminology

| Use | Not |
|---|---|
| Top Story | Breaking / Must-read |
| briefing | feed / digest (in UI; "digest" is fine in code) |
| Learn (nav) / Microlearning (section) | Lessons / Courses |
| US–India immigration | US-India / USA-India |

## Compliance note

Microlearning cards must be **original** (the build prompt forbids quoting or summarizing
specific copyrighted books). If the app is ever shared publicly, add an "AI-summarized"
note to the one-line "why it matters" lines.
